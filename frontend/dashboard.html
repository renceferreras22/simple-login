GOTHAM NETWORK

Welcome to Gotham

A city that never sleeps. Watch from the shadows, strike from above, and protect those who can't protect themselves.

Combat Styles

Freeflow Combat

Chain strikes and counters to take down a crowd without a pause.

Predator

Stay out of sight. Pick off armed thugs one at a time from the vents.

Detective Mode

Scan the scene to find clues, weak points, and hidden paths.

Known Threats

The Joker

Unpredictable and always laughing. Every plan has a punchline.

Scarecrow

Turns your fears against you. Keep your head clear.

Bane

Strength built from venom. Dodge first, then strike.

Riddler

Leaves puzzles across the city. Solve them all to reach him.

Field Notes

  • Use the Batclaw to pull down vent covers and weak walls.
  • Glide between gargoyles to stay above armed guards.
  • Counter when the warning icon flashes above an enemy.
  • Scan every crime scene before you move on.
  • Upgrade your gadgets between missions.
