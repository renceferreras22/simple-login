Welcome to Minecraft

A world made of blocks. Mine, build, and survive. Your only limit is your imagination.

Game Modes

Survival

Gather resources, craft tools, and stay alive through the night.

Creative

Unlimited blocks and flight. Build anything you can imagine.

Adventure

Play custom maps made by other players, with rules to follow.

Mobs You Will Meet

Creeper

Silent and green. It hisses, then explodes. Listen carefully.

Zombie

Wanders at night and burns in sunlight. Keep a sword handy.

Enderman

Tall and calm until you look at it. Do not make eye contact.

Villager

Friendly trader. Offer emeralds and get useful items back.

Beginner Tips

  • Punch a tree to get wood on your first day.
  • Build a shelter before the first night.
  • Never dig straight down. You might land in lava.
  • Keep a bed nearby to set your spawn point.
  • Bring torches when you explore caves.
